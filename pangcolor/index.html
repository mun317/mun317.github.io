PANG COLOR: Space Crystal Puzzle

Link same-color crystals in one swipe to blast enemy fleets! A 60-second puzzle shooter.

✨ One swipe, one blast! Link crystals of the same color to fire your laser

PANG COLOR is a puzzle shooter on a hexagon board. Draw one long line through crystals of the same color to attack — the longer and faster you chain, the stronger your fighter's laser gets.

How many stages can you break through in 60 seconds?

🚀 How to play

  • Link 3 or more same-color crystals in one stroke to attack
  • Keep chaining for combos and bigger damage
  • Fill the gauge for FEVER — double damage for 6 seconds
  • Link 6 or more to create a bomb that clears the area
  • Each enemy you destroy brings the next one — push as far as you can in 60 seconds

🌌 8 worlds, 80 stages

  • New enemy fleets in every world, from the outer asteroid belt to a ruined station
  • A giant boss waits at the end of each world
  • Incoming asteroids and crystal-locking EMPs make every world tougher

🛠️ Upgrade and evolve your fighter

  • Upgrade 4 modules: laser, armor, energy core and time booster
  • Upgrade them evenly and your fighter evolves through 10 forms
  • Charge your energy core to unleash a powerful skill

📖 A space adventure told in comics

  • Pilot Rin, the AI Pix, and Nox, commander of the Void Fleet
  • 16 comic episodes unfold as you clear each world

🎵 Upbeat chiptune music for every scene

※ Free to play with optional rewarded ads only (hearts, continue, daily credits). No in-app purchases.

※ Play in English, Japanese or Korean.
